Privacy Policy

Sentinex Vault

Developed by HeetDevLab

Effective Date: 11 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sentinex Vault (the "Application"), developed and operated by HeetDevLab ("we", "our", or "us"), handles information when you use the Application.

Sentinex Vault is designed to help users store and access private files, including photos, videos, audio files, documents, and other supported content, within a protected area of their device.

This policy describes the Application's data practices, permissions, local storage, security features, advertising, and your privacy choices.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, Sentinex Vault may handle the following information:

  • Private files: Photos, videos, audio recordings, PDFs, text files, and other files that you explicitly choose to import.
  • File information: File names, file types, sizes, timestamps, and other metadata needed to organize and display your files.
  • Vault security information: Vault settings, PIN-related verification data, favorites, Trash information, and other settings required for the Application to function.
  • Biometric authentication: If supported and enabled, the Application may request Android biometric authentication to help you unlock the vault.
  • Temporary preview data: Temporary files or cached content may be created when displaying photos, videos, audio, or documents.

We do not ask users to provide their private files to HeetDevLab as a condition of using the core vault features.

2. How Private Files Are Stored

Files imported into the vault are intended to be stored within the Application's private storage on your device. The Application's storage and encryption implementation determines how individual files and associated metadata are protected.

Where encryption is implemented, it is intended to help protect vault content against unauthorized access. Encryption does not guarantee protection against every possible attack or a compromised device.

HeetDevLab does not operate a cloud storage service for uploading or synchronizing your vault files.

Important: Do not uninstall the Application or clear its data without first considering the consequences. Depending on the storage and encryption design, doing so may permanently remove vault files or make them inaccessible.

3. File Import, Export, and Deletion

Sentinex Vault accesses files that you select through supported Android file selection interfaces or other explicitly enabled file-access features.

  • Import: When you import a file, the Application copies or processes the selected content into vault storage.
  • Original files: Depending on the import option presented and the action you confirm, the original file may remain in its original location or may be deleted.
  • Export: If you export a file, a copy may be written outside the vault to the destination you select.
  • Delete and Trash: Depending on the action you choose, files may be moved to the Application's Trash or permanently deleted.

Files exported outside the vault may no longer receive the protection provided by the vault's private storage. You are responsible for keeping exported copies secure.

4. Permissions We Use

The Application may request Android permissions or system-mediated access required for the features you use. The permissions requested by your installed version may differ depending on Android version and device capabilities.

  • Photo and video access: Used when you select or access media that you want to import into the vault.
  • Audio access: Used to select, import, and play supported audio files.
  • Document and file access: Used to select, import, display, or export supported documents and other files.
  • Biometric authentication: Used, when supported and enabled, to request system authentication before granting access to the vault. Biometric matching is handled by Android and the device's authentication system. The Application is not intended to receive your fingerprint image.
  • Notifications: If requested and enabled, notifications may be used for relevant Application functionality.
  • Other system permissions: Any additional permissions are used only for the corresponding features implemented in the installed version of the Application.

Android may allow you to grant, deny, or revoke certain permissions through system settings. Revoking a permission may limit the related feature.

5. Biometric Security

If biometric unlock is available and enabled, Sentinex Vault may invoke Android's system biometric prompt to authenticate you before opening protected content.

Biometric information, such as fingerprint templates, is managed by the device's operating system and secure authentication components. Sentinex Vault does not intentionally collect or upload fingerprint images or biometric templates.

Biometric authentication is an access-control feature. Its availability and security depend on your device, Android configuration, and the Application's implementation.

6. Advertising and Google AdMob

If advertising is enabled in your installed version, Sentinex Vault may use Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and advertising-related information, subject to the applicable advertising configuration, consent requirements, and Google's policies.

Advertising-related data processing is governed by the applicable third-party terms and privacy policies. It is separate from the vault's local file storage.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

7. Third-Party Services

The Application may rely on third-party components or services for specific functionality. The services actually included in a particular release may differ.

Where Google AdMob is integrated, Google may process information as described in its applicable privacy policy. We encourage users to review third-party privacy information relevant to the services enabled in their version.

8. Data Sharing

HeetDevLab does not sell users' private vault files. Sentinex Vault is not designed to upload private vault content to a HeetDevLab-operated cloud server.

Information may be processed by third-party services when their features are used, such as advertising services. Such services operate under their applicable policies.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or valid legal process, or where disclosure is otherwise permitted or required by applicable law.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Files and related information stored locally by Sentinex Vault generally remain on your device until you remove them, clear Application data, uninstall the Application, or the relevant storage is otherwise changed or deleted.

Files in Trash may remain stored until you restore them or permanently delete them, depending on the implemented Trash functionality.

Temporary preview files may be removed by the Application as part of its cache-management process.

Third-party services may retain information according to their own policies and applicable legal requirements.

10. Data Security

We aim to use reasonable technical measures to protect information handled by the Application. Depending on the installed version, these measures may include private application storage, encryption, PIN protection, and Android biometric authentication.

No application, storage system, or authentication method can guarantee absolute security. You should keep your device updated and avoid sharing your PIN or other authentication credentials.
